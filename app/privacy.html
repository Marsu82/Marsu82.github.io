Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026. The German version (Datenschutzerklärung) is authoritative.

Summary

Flugbahn does not collect personal data. There is no user account, no advertising and no analytics or tracking services. Almost everything runs offline on your device. Data only leaves the device in the cases listed below, and only when you start the respective function yourself.

Data on your device

Profiles, your own bullets, targets, shot log, harvest log, ammo stock, magazine statistics, shot timer history, packing list, saved GPS points and settings are stored only locally in your device’s storage (browser storage). This data does not leave the device unless you export or share it yourself (“Backup”, “Share profile”, “Pair spotter”).

Camera

The camera is only used when you start a camera function: group from photo, ammo from photo, measuring the flag angle or mirage. Images are analysed on the device, not stored and not transmitted.

Microphone and speech

The microphone is only used when you start the shot counter, the shot timer or voice control. The shot counter and shot timer only evaluate loudness on the device and do not store recordings. Voice control uses the speech recognition of your operating system or browser. Depending on the device, the provider of that speech recognition (e.g. Google) may process the recording on its servers; its privacy policy applies. Spoken corrections are produced on the device.

Location and motion sensors

Only when you tap “Location (GPS)”, “Distance by GPS”, “Measure angle” or “Measure direction” does the app ask for access to location or motion sensors. The values are used on the device and stored only as input values in your data.

Weather by location

When you tap “Load weather”, the app sends your coordinates, rounded to about 100 m, to the weather service Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com) to fetch temperature, pressure and wind. The service technically sees your IP address. No other data is transmitted.

Bluetooth devices

The connection to your own devices (handheld, radio bridge, weather station, sensors) runs directly via Bluetooth between your device and the measuring device. No server is involved.

Text recognition (ammo from photo)

On first use the app loads the text recognition library (Tesseract.js) from jsDelivr (cdn.jsdelivr.net). The service technically sees your IP address. Recognition then runs on the device; the photo is not transmitted.

AI coach (optional)

The AI coach is switched off until you enter your own API key from the provider Anthropic. When you then ask a question, the app sends your question together with your active profile, the weather values, the solution, the group and the last 15 shot log entries, encrypted, to Anthropic PBC (anthropic.com) to generate an answer. Anthropic’s privacy policy applies. The API key is stored only on your device (if you choose “Remember on this device”) and is not part of the backup. The cost of requests is borne by the key holder. The Play Store version does not contain the AI coach.

Servers and logs

The app is delivered as a static website and runs offline after the first load. When loading and updating, your device fetches the app files from the web server. The hosting provider may briefly store the usual access logs (e.g. IP address, time). The app has no influence on this and does not evaluate these logs.

App and website are hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. St., San Francisco, CA 94107, USA, a subsidiary of Microsoft Corporation). GitHub processes the IP address to deliver the pages and protect against abuse, also in the USA. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in secure and cost-effective provision (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). GitHub is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. Details: GitHub’s privacy statement.

Website

The website uses no cookies, no analytics and no third-party embeds. The chosen language is only stored in your browser’s storage.
